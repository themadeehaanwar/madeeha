About me

I'm a writer. I've written poetry since I was a teenager, and in 2025 I self-published a collection, Vestiges. I studied English literature and then an MBA in Marketing, and the two shaped how I work: I read closely, I care about getting things right, and I like turning an idea into something people can actually use.

With my mother, I run two food brands in Kolkata, Fivian and Flavoura. I handle the costing, the design and the orders. I've also managed a store, designed for clients, taught abacus, and built a handful of apps with AI tools. This page brings all of it together.
